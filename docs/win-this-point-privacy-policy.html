Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Win This Point is published by Uptempo Enterprises LLC. This policy explains what the game stores and how we handle support messages.

Playing the game

You can play Win This Point without an account. Starter set progress and preferences are saved on your device.

What stays on your device

The game saves starter set progress and first-try wins so you can resume and replay sets. It also saves your sound and haptic settings. These records stay in the app’s local storage until you delete the app.

Remove your local data

Delete Win This Point from your iPhone to remove its local progress and preferences from that device.

Support and privacy questions

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and other sensitive information.

For help or privacy questions, email info@uptempoenterprises.com or visit game support.

Policy updates

Before enabling advertising, we’ll update this page to explain the ad provider, how ad-related information is used, and the privacy choices available in the app.
