Word Journal privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

Word Journal is published by Uptempo Enterprises LLC. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game or contact support.

The game

Word Journal is an offline word-puzzle app. The app does not collect or transmit your puzzle entries, progress, personal identifiers or usage information. It has no account system, ads, third-party analytics or external service integrations.

Saved progress

The app saves puzzle paths, unfinished entries, the selected puzzle, hint counts and completed-puzzle marks in its local app preferences. These values let you resume play on the same device. They stay until you reset a puzzle, erase all progress or remove the app. Use Help → Privacy & local data → Erase all progress to clear the journal's saved data on the device. Resetting one puzzle clears that attempt while keeping its historical completion mark.

Apple services

Local app data may be included in device backups according to your Apple backup settings. Deleting app data does not independently delete backups. Apple manages operating-system services and any diagnostics you choose to share under your Apple settings; those services are separate from data collection by Word Journal.

Permissions and tracking

The app does not sell data or share it with advertising or analytics providers. It does not request access to contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera. There are no advertising or behavioral-tracking identifiers in the app.

Support messages

If you email support, Uptempo Enterprises LLC receives your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information. You can ask about deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the game's practices change. For questions about Word Journal or this policy, contact info@uptempoenterprises.com. Support is available at Uptempo support.
