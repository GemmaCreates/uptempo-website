Word Journal privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Word Journal is published by Uptempo Enterprises LLC. This policy explains how information is handled in Word Journal, including builds that use Google advertising. Earlier ad-free builds do not make the advertising requests described below. The current ad-enabled TestFlight build uses Google sample advertisements, not live advertising inventory.

Your journal

Puzzles work offline. Puzzle paths, unfinished entries, selected journey, hint counts, completion marks and your instruction-screen preference are stored in local app preferences. Word Journal does not attach puzzle entries or saved paths to advertising requests or support reports. There is no Word Journal account, subscription or paid puzzle unlock.

Use How to play → Privacy & local data → Erase all progress to clear the journal's local saved data after confirmation. Resetting a puzzle clears that attempt while keeping its historical completion mark. Local data may be included in Apple device backups according to your settings. Erasing progress does not delete Apple backups, information held by Google or support correspondence.

Google advertising and consent services

Ad-enabled builds include Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. A banner may appear after a completed puzzle. Hints, Undo, replay and Next remain free, and you can continue when offline or when an ad does not load. Sample advertisements still involve requests to Google services.

Google's SDK declarations identify categories including device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, approximate location, crash information, performance information and other diagnostics. The consent SDK also declares approximate location, performance and product interaction information. These services may process this information to operate and measure advertising and consent services. Information can include ordinary network data such as an IP address. Google's practices are described in Google advertising privacy and Google's privacy policy.

Word Journal requests non-personalized advertising and disables automatic SDK approximate-location collection. These settings do not mean Google receives no identifiers or network information, and are not a promise that no approximate location can be derived from an IP address. We do not include a separate first-party analytics service; advertising and consent SDKs may process their own diagnostic and interaction information.

Advertising choices

The current sample-ad build does not use the publisher's live advertising consent messages. In a build configured for live advertising, Word Journal uses Google's consent service to request the applicable privacy choices before eligible ad requests. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Advertising privacy choices is available in Privacy & local data. Your ability to solve and save puzzles does not depend on consenting to personalized ads. There is no paid ad-removal product or general ads-off switch in the current version.

Word Journal does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device location. It does not request an advertising tracking permission in the current sample-ad build. This does not mean the included advertising SDK processes no device identifiers. You can also manage relevant Apple privacy and backup settings on your device.

Ad concerns and support

The sample-ad report form is a practice: completing it does not send a report to Google or Uptempo. A direct live-reporting service is not enabled in the current version. If you have a concern about an ad, email info@uptempoenterprises.com. You may include the ad reference shown in the app, the app version, your reason and a description. Please avoid sensitive information and puzzle entries.

Where the app offers Open email draft, it prepares a message for your review in your mail app. The draft includes a report reference, Word Journal's bundle identifier and version, the ad reference, reason and optional explanation. Your email address and any edits or attachments you choose to add are included if you send the message. Opening a draft is not confirmation that support received it. If a mail app cannot open, the app provides report details you can copy. Older builds can use the support email link or contact address directly.

Uptempo receives support messages you choose to send and uses them to respond to and investigate the concern. Your mail provider and the receiving mail service process the message and connection information under their own practices. The app does not automatically attach saved puzzle paths, screenshots, location data or advertising consent strings. Ask about access to or deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. Clearing local journal progress does not delete an email you have sent.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app's practices change, including before enabling a different reporting service. For privacy questions, contact info@uptempoenterprises.com. Support is available at Uptempo support.
