First Clue privacy policy

This policy explains how Uptempo Enterprises handles information in First Clue for iPhone.

Last updated November 26, 2026.

What the app saves

First Clue saves your letter guesses, current selection, current puzzle, and completion history on your device so you can resume. You can play without an account.

Advertising and privacy choices

Some versions of First Clue are ad-free. In versions with advertising enabled, a Google AdMob banner may appear after you solve a puzzle. All puzzles and clues are free. Your choice about ads does not change what you can play. First Clue keeps your guesses and saved puzzle progress on your device and does not include them in ad requests.

If advertising is enabled, Google's advertising and consent tools may process your IP address, general location inferred from your IP address, device or app identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may use information to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain their services. First Clue requests non-personalized ads. Google may still process information for these other purposes. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy for more about its practices and controls.

Where a consent form is required, it explains the available choices and identifies the participating providers. You can decline consent. When Advertising privacy choices is available, you can review or change your choices in Puzzle options → Privacy. Depending on your choices and location, you may see limited ads or no ads.

Removing your progress

Your progress stays on the device while the app is installed. Replaying a puzzle clears that puzzle's guesses and keeps its completion record. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and the details you include. We use that information to respond and keep the correspondence as needed to handle your request. For a privacy question or to request deletion of your support correspondence, email info@uptempoenterprises.com.
