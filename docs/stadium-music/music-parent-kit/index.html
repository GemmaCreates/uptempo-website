Before you take the music phone

A setup check for the adult running walk-up music.

  • ☐ Active Apple Music subscription. Catalog playback needs it.
  • ☐ Internet at the field. Check it on the device you’ll use.
  • ☐ Phone and speaker tested together. Rehearse start, stop and volume before play.
  • ☐ Field rules confirmed. Ask when music may play and which lyrics are allowed.

Rehearse the handoff

  1. Collect the choices. Write down the player names and song choices. Confirm clean-language expectations and field rules with the team.
  2. Check the connection. Confirm an active Apple Music subscription and internet access on the music device. Charge the phone and test the same speaker you plan to use.
  3. Start with four players. Set up the first four players, assign their songs and choose each clip's start and length. Preview at a comfortable volume.
  4. Rehearse the handoff. Open the Gameday screen. Practice starting and stopping the right player's clip. Show the backup volunteer the controls on the same device; do not assume the roster transfers to another phone.
