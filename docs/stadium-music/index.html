Set it up before the game

  1. Add up to four players free. Keep their names in your lineup.
  2. Choose their Apple Music songs. Set the start time and clip length.
  3. Test your speaker. Check the connection and volume before using the game-day controls.

Before you download

Does it work with Spotify?

Stadium Music uses Apple Music. You’ll need an active Apple Music subscription.

What’s free?

You can set up one team with four players and try song selection, clip editing and game-day playback. Unlimited players cost $0.99 once. The Apple Music subscription is separate.

Can our team play music at the field?

Check with your league or tournament before game day. Ask when music may play and who controls the speaker.
