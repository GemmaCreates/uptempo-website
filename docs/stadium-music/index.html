Before you download

Does it work with Spotify?

Stadium Music uses Apple Music. You’ll need an active Apple Music subscription.

What’s free?

One team with four players, including song selection, clip editing, and game-day playback. Add unlimited players for $0.99, paid once. Your Apple Music subscription is separate.

Can our team play music at the field?

Check with your league or tournament before game day. Ask when music may play and who controls the speaker.
