Fresh Fade

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Fresh Fade: Build a Barbershop is published by Uptempo Enterprises LLC. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game.

Your game

Fresh Fade does not require an account. Shop progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate a game account system or a server that receives your saved shop progress.

Purchases

Fresh Fade offers one optional, non-consumable purchase: Remove Ads. Apple processes purchases, restores, and refunds through the App Store. We receive only the entitlement information needed to decide whether ads should be removed in the game. We do not receive payment-card details.

Ads

The game may show an ad, including a native ad with image or video content, only between business days. It never shows an ad during a haircut, shop decision, or other interactive task. The game requests child-directed and under-age treatment, uses a general-audience content rating, disables publisher first-party ID, and disables ad personalization in its Google Mobile Ads configuration. The game does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or use the IDFA for tracking.

Google Mobile Ads may process information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and improve ads. This can include an IP address, which can estimate general location; non-user-related crash logs; performance data; device identifiers; advertising data; and ad or product interaction data. Google may use those categories for advertising, analytics, and SDK operations. We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data, health data, or payment information.

Privacy choices

Where required, the Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads are requested. When privacy options are available, Fresh Fade provides a Privacy choices entry in Settings so players can revisit those choices. Selecting Remove Ads permanently prevents ads from being requested by the game.

Children and families

Fresh Fade is designed to be approachable for a broad audience. The game uses child-directed and under-age treatment flags for its ad and consent requests, and places purchases behind a grown-up gate. Parents or guardians can use the privacy choices entry in Settings where it is shown, or contact us with questions.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request. Please do not send payment-card details or other sensitive information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or applicable requirements change. We will post the current version at this page.

For questions about Fresh Fade or this policy, contact info@uptempoenterprises.com.
