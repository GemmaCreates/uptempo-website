Privacy policy

Updated November 18, 2026
Uptempo Enterprises LLC

Your game

Wide World Golf stores your current round, scores, personal bests and coaching preference in the app’s storage on your device. No account is required. We do not operate a game server or receive your saved rounds.

No ads or tracking

The game contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, in-app purchases or social features. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Device backups

Your device’s backup and restore services may include app data according to your Apple settings. We do not operate those services or receive your device backups.

Your choices

You can retry a hole or start another round after the scorecard. To remove the app’s local game data, delete the app and its data through iOS Settings. A device backup may restore earlier data.

Support messages

If you email info@uptempoenterprises.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Opening the support website or your mail app is optional and uses those services separately from gameplay.

Changes and contact

Wide World Golf is published by Uptempo Enterprises LLC. We may update this policy as the app changes. For questions about the game or this policy, contact info@uptempoenterprises.com.
