Wide World of Golf

Privacy policy

Updated November 20, 2026
Uptempo Enterprises LLC

Your game

Wide World of Golf stores your current round, scores, personal bests and coaching preference in the app’s storage on your device. No account is required. We do not receive your saved rounds.

Advertising

If you play with ads, Google AdMob may show an ad after a completed hole or round. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad views and interactions, and performance and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain its services. Google may use your IP address to determine a general area for regional privacy choices. We configure ads without personalization and the game does not request app tracking permission or access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Ad privacy choices

Where regional privacy law requires it, the game offers Ad privacy choices in Help. The available choices depend on your region. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Remove Ads

Apple processes the one-time Remove Ads purchase. The game checks your purchase with Apple and you can use Restore Purchases in Help to recover access. We do not receive your payment details. The game stops requesting and showing ads after it verifies that you own this purchase.

Device backups

Your device’s backup and restore services may include app data according to your Apple settings. We do not operate those services or receive your device backups.

Your choices

You can retry a hole or start another round after the scorecard. To remove the app’s local game data, delete the app and its data through iOS Settings. A device backup may restore earlier data.

Course votes and in-app feedback

In versions with course voting or in-app feedback, Uptempo receives the choices you submit, any note you add, the app version and build, and the current hole for a feedback report. We use this information to plan courses, investigate reports and improve the game. We record when the response arrived and use a random submission code to prevent duplicate retries. Our feedback hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address to limit spam. We do not save your IP address with the response or use responses for advertising or tracking. Responses are deleted from the live inbox after about 180 days. Hosting backups can retain deleted responses for up to 30 more days. Please leave out personal or sensitive information.

Support messages

If you email info@uptempoenterprises.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Opening the support website or your mail app is optional and uses those services separately from gameplay.

Changes and contact

Wide World of Golf is published by Uptempo Enterprises LLC. We may update this policy as the app changes. For questions about the game or this policy, contact info@uptempoenterprises.com.
